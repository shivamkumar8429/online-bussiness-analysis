2. Overall Description

2.1 Business Problem

An online shopping company receives orders across five cities, four product categories and five payment methods, and customers rate each order. The operations manager does not know which factors are linked to low satisfaction or which orders need attention. This analysis shows how satisfaction is spread, how it relates to delivery time and order value, and which groups and orders are weakest, so the manager can decide where to improve delivery and follow up with unhappy customers.

2.2 Objectives

The project answers these four questions:

1.  What does the overall Satisfaction_Score look like?

2.  How are Delivery_Days and Order_Value_INR related to Satisfaction_Score?

3.  Does satisfaction differ by Product_Category, City, Payment_Method or Device_Type?

4.  Which orders need attention (At Risk: Satisfaction_Score below 60)?

2.5 Assumptions and Constraints

-   The dataset is synthetic and was created with an AI tool (Claude).

-   The raw dataset has about 255 rows and the cleaned dataset has 245 rows, so the results show patterns in this data only.

-   Valid ranges: Customer_Age 18 to 100, Delivery_Days 0 or more, Discount_Percent 0 to 100, Satisfaction_Score 0 to 100.

-   At Risk is defined as Satisfaction_Score below 60. This is a project rule, not an industry standard.

-   Order_Value_INR may be before or after discount; the source does not say, so the discount amount is an estimate.

-   Correlation shows association, not cause.
