SLIDE 3

Problem Statement

3

Data Analyst Training Project

Business Problem

The company received 250 orders in 5 cities, but the average
satisfaction score is only 70.6 out of 100. The manager does not know
what pulls the score down: slow delivery, low discounts, or the payment
method. The analysis will help decide where to spend money first to
raise satisfaction and which product category to focus on.

Stakeholder

Operations and Marketing Manager of an online shopping company.

Why It Matters

Happy customers buy again. Fixing slow delivery and using discounts well
can lift satisfaction and protect sales.

Data Quality Issues and Cleaning

6

Data Analyst Training Project

Column | Problem found | How many | How I fixed it, and why

Order_ID (all columns) | Duplicate rows | 5 | Removed the repeated rows,
because they count the same order twice.

Payment_Method, Age, Delivery_Days, Satisfaction | Missing values | 20 |
Median for numbers, “Unknown” for payment method, so no rows are lost.

City | Inconsistent text | 7 | Removed extra spaces and used one
spelling, so “kolkata” and “KOLKATA” become Kolkata.

Discount_Percent | Impossible values (250 and 135) | 2 | A discount
cannot be above 100%, so I treated them as typing errors and used the
median discount.

Question 1: Does slow delivery hurt satisfaction?

8

Data Analyst Training Project

What the Chart Shows

Average satisfaction score (up) for each delivery time in days (across).
The line goes down as days increase.

Finding

Orders delivered in 1-3 days score 75.7, but orders taking 7+ days score
only 63.0. That is a 12.7 point gap.

SLIDE 14

Challenges and Learnings

14

Data Analyst Training Project

Challenges I Faced

• City names had different spellings and spaces; I fixed them with
strip() and title(). • Two discounts were above 100% (250 and 135); I
found them with describe() and replaced them. • Choosing how to fill 20
blank values took time; I used the median and “Unknown”.

What I Learnt

• I can now find duplicates, blanks and wrong values in pandas and group
data with groupby(). • Delivery time matters a lot in online shopping:
its link with satisfaction is -0.59. • Next time I would test whether
discounts really cause higher satisfaction, with more data.
